About Me

Background

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, with a focus on data analytics, data engineering, and software development. I enjoy working at the intersection of building things that work and understanding what the data says.

My experience spans software engineering, geospatial analytics, cloud infrastructure, and data-intensive applications. At NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, I've completed two internships: most recently building shift-left testing infrastructure and tuning a machine learning anomaly detection model for satellite telemetry across active Earth science missions, and before that developing a Python reporting pipeline and cloud data workflows for the SWOT satellite. At University of Chicago Urban Labs, I developed analytical and geospatial workflows for large public-sector datasets, combining data engineering, visualization, and reporting to support research initiatives. Earlier internships at Aneyeon and the Haitian Global Health Alliance gave me a foundation in full-stack development and applied data analysis. Across these experiences, I've become interested in building reliable systems around complex real-world data.

Outside of internships and coursework, I enjoy creating projects that sit somewhere between engineering and experimentation, whether that means building interactive tools, exploring new technologies, or finding cleaner ways to organize and visualize information. I’m particularly drawn to projects where software can simplify complicated processes, support better decisions, or make technical ideas more accessible to people.
